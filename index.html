What you are looking at

Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and “Reset data” puts it back to the seeded start. The workflow rules are the real ones — who may approve what, who may see a budget, who may work a door — so if a page refuses you, that is the rule and not a missing button.
