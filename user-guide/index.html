Culturals — User guide

Culturals is the college's clubs and events app: students find events and get in with a QR pass, clubs propose and run events with a budget, and the Cultural Society, Dean and Vice-Chancellor approve them.

Start with Getting started, then open the chapter for your role.

Approval flow

flowchart TD
    A[Club proposes<br/>event + budget] --> B{Cultural or<br/>literary club?}
    B -- yes --> CS[Cultural Society]
    B -- no --> D[Dean of<br/>Student Affairs]
    CS -- approve --> D
    D -- approve --> V{Over ₹50,000<br/>or part of a fest?}
    V -- yes --> VC[Vice-Chancellor]
    V -- no --> P([Published])
    VC -- approve --> P
    CS & D & VC -. return for changes .-> E[Club edits<br/>and resubmits]
    E -.-> B
    CS & D & VC -. reject .-> R([Rejected])

Every approver can Approve, Return for changes (the club edits and sends it again, and it starts from the first gate) or Reject (final). Returning and rejecting need a comment. The Dean can sanction less than a budget line asks for, never more.

After it is published: students register and get a pass → on the day, organisers scan passes at the door (Live) → afterwards the organiser closes the event (Reported) and the treasurer enters actual spend and receipts, then settles the budget.

The demo data has one event in each state, so you can see them all: Folk Fusion Evening (pending with the Dean), Carnatic Music Recital (approved and published), Late-Night Improv Marathon (rejected by the Dean) and Short Film Screening (returned by the Dean, edited by the club and resubmitted).

Screenshots were taken on the demo server with its seeded sample data (names, clubs and events are fictional).
