Who does what

For all office bearers

An event goes through five stages. Each one leaves a record.

Proposeevent, document, budget
ApprovalSociety, Dean, sometimes the VC
Publishedstudents register
Event daypasses scanned
Reportedreport, bills, settled

What each office bearer can do

TaskPresidentVice-PresidentSecretaryTreasurerPage
Propose an event6
Attach the proposal document7
Build the budget14
Enter spend, attach bills, settle15
Scan passes at the door · core team too16
Go live, send the post-event report18
Calendar and the Wednesday open block13
Assign roles, accept join requests19

Nobody approves their own club's proposal. Approvers do, and you are told what they decide.

Nobody in the club moves a sent event. Only the Cultural Society and the Dean can.

Plain members see events and the roster, never budgets or the proposal.

Part 2 · Proposing an event6

The budget and the proposal document

For all office bearers

Every proposal carries a document that explains it and a budget that prices it.

A template

Performance, Workshop, Competition or Exhibition, priced from your expected attendance.

Upload the budget sheet

A CSV: head, amount. Download the budget template for the layout.

The Treasurer enters it

Choose None. Saved as a draft; priced on the Budget page.

Preview the sheet before sending

  1. 1
    Each row is one budget line. One wrong row and nothing is imported; you are told which.

The proposal document

Who reads it. Your office bearers, the Cultural Society, the Dean, the Vice-Chancellor. Never students.

In the app. Click the file name: a PDF opens in the page, a .docx shows as text.

Locked once sent. Replace it only while the event is a draft or returned.

Rejected

For all office bearers

Final. It cannot be edited or sent again. To try again, propose a new event.

  1. 1
    Why · the comment is required
  1. 2
    Delete removes it from your list. Optional.

Rejected at a later step

  1. 1
    The Dean's cut still shows, in violet
  2. 2
    The Vice-Chancellor's reason

Returned means "fix this and send it again". Rejected means "no". The venue slot is released.

Quick answers

For all office bearers
I can't find Send for approval.
The budget has no lines. Add them, or declare it nil.
Can I change the date after sending?
No. Ask the Cultural Society (while it is with them) or the Dean.
The venue or time changed.
An approver moved it. Notifications says who and why.
My budget is smaller than I asked.
The Dean lowered a line. The budget page shows which.
A student has no phone.
Type their PRN at the scan station.
The network dropped at the door.
Keep scanning. Scans are sent when it returns.

Tell us what to improve

This is a pilot. Note the page and what you expected, and pass it on to the Culturals team.
