Who does what

For all office bearers

A club runs an event in four stages. Each stage leaves a record, so the semester report is built from the app and nobody collects it by hand.

Proposeevent, proposal document, budget
ApprovalCultural Society, Dean, sometimes the Vice-Chancellor
Publishedstudents register and get a pass
Event daypasses scanned at the door
Reportedreport in, bills attached, budget settled

What each office bearer can do

TaskPresidentVice-PresidentSecretaryTreasurerSee page
Propose an event · and send it for approval6
Attach the proposal document7
Build the budget · lines or an uploaded sheet14
Enter spend, attach bills, settle15
Go live and close the event · the post-event report18
Scan passes at the door · core team too16
See the calendar, book the Wednesday open block13
Assign roles, accept join requests19

Nobody in the club approves the club's own proposal, and nobody in the club can move an event's time or venue once it is sent. The approvers do that, and you are told when they do.

Plain members see the club's events and roster. They never see budgets or the proposal document.

Sign in and find your way

For all office bearers

Use your college email and password. Office bearers do not need an authenticator code; only approvers do.

  1. 1
    Type your institutional email.
  2. 2
    Type your password.
  3. 3
    Click Sign in.

On the pilot: every account's password is culturals-demo. The sign-in page lists one pilot account per role.

Your menu

The Club part of the menu is what this guide covers. This is a President's menu; you see only the pages your role has.

  1. 1
    Club events: every event your club has proposed. You will use this most.
  2. 2
    New event: propose one.
  3. 3
    Budget: every budget, from draft to settled. Not for the Secretary.
  4. 4
    Members & roles: the roster. Presidents and Vice-Presidents assign roles.
  5. 5
    Join requests: students waiting to join. President and Vice-President.
  6. 6
    Scan station: the door on event day.
  7. 7
    Calendar: every venue booking. President and Vice-President.

The pages above Club (Feed, What's on, My passes, Profile QR, Clubs) are the student pages everyone has.

Club events

For all office bearers

Every event your club has proposed, newest first.

  1. 1
    New event starts a proposal.
  2. 3
    Send for approval appears on a draft or a returned proposal once it has a budget.
  1. 4
    Open shows the event's page: its approval trail, budget, proposal document and report.

The two labels on a card

The first is the event's status (Draft, Awaiting approval, Published…). The second is the budget's stage (With the Cultural Society, Sanctioned, Settled…).

The tabs

Drafts are not sent yet. With approvers are waiting for a decision. Upcoming are approved. Past have run.

The bar

How many students have registered, out of the capacity, and what was requested.

Wednesday open block President · Vice-President

The card at the top of the page. Choose a venue for the 3–4 pm or the 4–5 pm hour and click Book: it is yours at once. No proposal, no budget, no approval. First come, first served, and one slot per club each Wednesday.

2Proposing an event
Part 2 · Proposing an event5

Propose a new event

For all office bearers

One form. Fill it top to bottom, attach the proposal, choose how the budget comes in, then send it or save it as a draft.

  1. 1
    Title, and the kind of event.
  2. 2
    Venue. Each option shows how many it seats.
  3. 3
    Start date and hours. Venues are booked in whole hours.
  4. 4
    Capacity fills in from the venue you chose. Lower it if you are admitting fewer.
  5. 5
    Budget. Pick a template, Upload the budget sheet (CSV), or leave it for the Treasurer.
  6. 6
    Budget sheet. Appears when you choose to upload.
  7. 7
    Proposal document. A PDF or Word file, up to 10 MB.
  8. 8
    Propose and send sends it for approval. With no budget chosen the button reads Save draft.

If two events want the same hall

A slot another club has already been approved for is refused. A slot another club has only asked for is allowed, and the approvers see the clash. One of the two will be moved.

2Proposing an event
Part 2 · Proposing an event6

The budget and the proposal document

For all office bearers

A proposal is sent with a document that explains it and a budget that prices it.

Three ways to give the budget

A template

Choose Performance, Workshop, Competition or Exhibition. The lines are priced from your expected attendance. Fastest.

Upload the budget sheet

A CSV with the columns head, amount (and optionally basis). Download the budget template gives you the layout. President, Vice-President, Treasurer.

The Treasurer enters it

Choose None. The event is saved as a draft and the Treasurer adds the lines on the Budget page (page 14).

Check the sheet before you send it

Click Preview the sheet to read your CSV as a table before anything is uploaded.

  1. 1
    Each row becomes one budget line.

Every row is checked. If one row is wrong, nothing is imported, the event is kept as a draft, and you are told which row to fix.

The proposal document

Who reads it

Your club's office bearers, the Cultural Society, the Dean and the Vice-Chancellor. Students and plain members never see it.

Reading it in the app

Click the file name on the event page. A PDF opens in the page; a Word .docx shows as its text. An old .doc can only be downloaded.

Changing it

Replace it while the event is a draft or returned. Once sent, it is locked, so approvers read what you sent.

2Proposing an event
Part 2 · Proposing an event7

Following your proposal

For all office bearers

Open the event from Club events. The page tells you where the proposal is and what happened to it.

  1. 1
    Status. Awaiting approval means an approver has it.
  2. 2
    When and where. If an approver moved it, the old value is struck through (page 10).
  3. 3
    Open budget shows every line and what was sanctioned.
  4. 4
    Proposal document. Click the file name to read it.

The approval route

Clubyou send it
Cultural Societyits Secretary or Vice-Secretary
Deanevery event; may lower a budget line
Vice-Chancelloronly over ₹50,000 or part of a fest
Publishedstudents can register

Each approver can approve, return for changes, or reject. To return or reject they must write a comment, and you see it.

While it is with an approver you cannot edit it. That way they approve exactly what they read.

3Approval and changes
Part 3 · Approval and changes8

Notifications

For all office bearers

Every decision and every change an approver makes reaches you twice: in the app, and by email to your institutional address.

  1. 1
    The bell in the top bar shows how many you have not read. Click it.
  1. 1
    Colour key. Violet A is a change by the Cultural Society or the Dean. Teal M is the Vice-Chancellor.
  2. 2
    Filters. Decisions, or only Changes by approvers.
  3. 3
    Venue or time changed. The new slot, the old one, the reason, and who moved it.
  4. 4
    Rejected, with the approver's comment.
  5. 5
    Returned for changes, with what to fix.

New marks what you had not read. Open it on a notification takes you to the event. Decisions go to the President, Vice-President and Treasurer; a move also goes to the Secretary and to every student who registered.

3Approval and changes
Part 3 · Approval and changes9

When an approver changes something

For all office bearers

Approvers can change two things: when and where the event is, and how much money it gets. The old value is struck through and the new one is in the approver's colour.

A new venue or time Cultural Society officers · Dean

  1. 1
    When and where shows the old slot struck out and the new one beside it.
  2. 2
    Changes by approvers lists each change: what it was, what it is now.
  3. 3
    The reason the approver gave.

The Society's Secretary and Vice-Secretary can move an event while it is with the Society. After that, only the Dean can. Students who registered are told too.

A smaller budget Dean

  1. 1
    Cultural Society approved, with a comment.
  2. 2
    The Dean lowered the total. Struck figure: what you asked. Violet figure: what you have.
  3. 3
    Vice-Chancellor approved. Their mark is the teal M.

The Dean can lower a line, never raise it. You get a notification naming each line that changed. The budget page shows the lines (page 15).

Returned for changes

For all office bearers

An approver wants something fixed. The proposal is yours again: edit it and send it back.

  1. 1
    Returned for changes: the status.
  2. 3
    Open budget to change the lines. Every field is open again.
  1. 4
    Replace the proposal document if the comment asks for it.
  2. 5
    Send for approval when it is fixed.

What they asked for

  1. 1
    The approver's comment is under their name in the Approval trail.

When you send it again it starts at the first step, so earlier approvers see the changed version. The trail keeps the returned request and adds the new one below it.

3Approval and changes
Part 3 · Approval and changes11

Rejected

For all office bearers

A rejection is final. The proposal cannot be edited or sent again. To try again, propose a new event.

  1. 1
    Why. The approver's comment is required, and it is shown here.
  1. 2
    Delete removes the rejected proposal from your list. Optional.

The event's status and budget both read Rejected. The venue slot is released at once, so another club can book it.

Rejected at a later step

Any approver on the route can reject. Here the Cultural Society and the Dean approved, and the Vice-Chancellor rejected.

  1. 1
    The Dean's change to the budget still shows, in violet.
  2. 2
    The Vice-Chancellor's reason.

Returned or rejected? Returned means "fix this and send it again". Rejected means "no".

The calendar

For Presidents and Vice-Presidents

Every venue booking in the college, so you can pick a free slot before you propose.

  1. 1
    Upcoming events: every booking from today on, marked Approved or Awaiting approval. Click one to open its day.
  1. 2
    Previous / Next month and week move the calendar.
  2. 3
    Go to date jumps to any day.
  1. 4
    The week. Each day says Free or how many events. Click a day to see it hour by hour, one column per venue.

What you see of other clubs

An approved event shows its name. Another club's event that is not approved yet shows only as Requested: the slot is taken, the idea stays private.

You cannot move a booking. Only the Dean, and the Cultural Society's officers while a proposal is with them, can.

3Approval and changes
Part 3 · Approval and changes13

Build the budget

For Treasurer · President · Vice-President

Open Budget, then the event. You can change the budget while the event is a draft or has been returned.

  1. 1
    Edit all turns every line into text boxes. Change several, save once.
  2. 2
    Edit or Remove one line.
  3. 3
    Add an entry: a head (what it is for) and an amount.
  1. 4
    Upload a CSV to add many lines at once. Choose whether it replaces the existing lines or is added to them.
  2. 5
    Download template for the CSV layout.
  3. 6
    Send for approval when the lines are right.

The three figures

Requested is what the club asks. Sanctioned is what the approvers allowed. Spent is what you record after the event.

The envelope

Your club's budget for the year, if the Dean has set one, with how much is already sanctioned and settled.

An event that costs nothing

With no priced lines, declare the budget nil and give the reason. It can then be sent without lines.

Over ₹50,000, or part of a fest, the proposal also goes to the Vice-Chancellor. The page tells you before you send it.

After approval: spend and receipts

For Treasurer · President · Vice-President

Once approved the budget is Sanctioned. The lines are fixed; after the event you record what was actually spent.

  1. 1
    Sanctioned is the money you may spend.
  2. 2
    A line the Dean lowered shows what you asked struck through, the new figure, and a violet A.

Lines left alone are sanctioned as requested.

Record the spend

  1. 1
    Spent: what the line actually cost.
  2. 2
    Note: anything worth recording. Required if the line went over.
  3. 3
    Save.
  1. 4
    Choose the bill (PDF or a photo).
  2. 5
    Attach. It appears under the line; click it to open.

A receipt is required for any line above ₹5,000. A line that went over what was sanctioned is marked Over sanctioned and needs a reason.

Settle budget, at the bottom, closes it. If something is missing, the page lists exactly which lines need a receipt or a reason. A settled budget cannot be changed.

Open the door

For President · Vice-President · Secretary · core team

On the day, the event page of a published event has two buttons.

  1. 1
    Go live when the event starts. Students see it as Live now.
  1. 2
    Scan station opens the door page on this device.

The scan station

  1. 1
    Event at this door. Check it is the right one.
  2. 2
    Start camera, then hold each student's pass or profile QR up to it.
  3. 3
    No camera? Type or paste the code under the QR and click Check code.
  4. 4
    No phone? Type the PRN from their ID card and click Check in by PRN.
  1. 5
    At the door: checked in, registered, capacity.
  2. 6
    Recent scans: the last few, with their result.

Use a phone or a laptop. Several people can scan at once on their own devices.

5Event day and after
Part 5 · Event day and after16

Check people in

For whoever is at the door

Every scan gets an answer in under a second. Green: let them in. Amber: look again. Red: do not admit on this code.

  1. 1
    The verdict names the student, so you can check the face against the ID card.
Checked in
A registered student. Let them in.
Registered and checked in
A walk-in who was not registered. They are now, and they are in.
Already checked in
This pass was used before; it says when. A pass works once.
Wrong event
A pass for another event. Ask for their profile QR instead.
Pass was replaced
An old copy. Ask them to open the current pass.
Event at capacity
A walk-in, but every place is taken.
Code not recognised
Not a Culturals code, or mistyped. Scan again or use the PRN.
Saved offline
No connection. The scan is kept on this device and sent when the network returns.

Who is in, who is not

  1. 1
    Find by name or PRN to check one person.

The filter shows All, only those Checked in, or those Not yet here. The counts on the event page come from these scans; nobody types attendance in.

The post-event report

For President · Vice-President · Secretary

Once the event is live, its page has a Post-event report. Fill it in after the event; it is what the semester report is built from.

  1. 1
    Checklist. What is still needed before you can submit.
  2. 2
    Attendance is already filled in from the door.
  1. 3
    Photos. At least one is required. Mark the best as a highlight.
  2. 4
    Social media posts. Paste the link to each post. Optional.
  1. 1
    Spent on each line.
  2. 2
    The bill for it.

Bills do not hold up the report. The budget cannot be settled without them.

  1. 1
    Outcomes (required): what happened, who came, what it achieved.
  2. 2
    Learnings: what to keep or change.
  3. 3
    Submit report. The event becomes Reported.

A student in a photo can ask for it to be taken down. You see the request on the photo and can remove it there.

Members, roles and join requests

For Presidents and Vice-Presidents

The roster is Members & roles. Every change of role is recorded with who made it.

  1. 1
    Role. Choose a member's role: Member, Core team, Secretary, Treasurer, Vice-President, President.
  1. 2
    Office bearers wear a gold ring.
  1. 3
    Open join requests when students are waiting.
  1. 4
    Handover. Name next term's office bearers and the date it takes effect. The outgoing holders lose their permissions at that time.

The Treasurer cannot assign roles. A student can be in up to five clubs.

Join requests

  1. 1
    Tick the students you want to decide together, or
  2. 2
    Accept one student.
  1. 3
    Decline one student.
  2. 4
    Approve selected accepts everyone you ticked.

Quick answers

For all office bearers
I can't find Send for approval.
The budget has no lines yet. Add them, or declare the budget nil, then send.
Can I change the date after sending?
No. Ask the Cultural Society (while it is with them) or the Dean to move it.
The venue or time changed and I did not do it.
An approver moved it. Open Notifications or the event page for who and why.
My budget is smaller than I asked.
The Dean lowered a line. The budget page shows which, struck through.
A student has no phone at the door.
Type their PRN into the scan station.
The network dropped at the door.
Keep scanning. Scans are saved on the device and sent when it is back.

Tell us what to improve

This is a pilot. If a page is confusing or a step is missing, note the page and what you expected, and pass it on to the Culturals team.
