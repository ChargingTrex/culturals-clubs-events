How a proposal is approved

For all approvers

Clubs propose events with a budget in Culturals. Every proposal passes the approvers below before students can see it.

Clubproposes the event and budget
Cultural Societycultural and literary clubs only
Deanevery event, always
Vice-Chancelloronly over ₹50,000 or part of a fest
Publishedstudents can register

Three decisions, at every step

Approve

Sends it to the next approver, or publishes it if you are the last one.

Return for changes

Back to the club to fix. When they send it again, it starts again at the first step.

Reject

Closed for good. It can't be sent again.

A comment is required to return or reject. The club sees it. Approving needs no comment.

48 hours per step. A proposal that waits longer is marked Past SLA.

Sign in

For all approvers

Approvers commit college money, so you need a 6-digit code from your authenticator app as well as your password.

  1. 1
    Type your college email and password, then click I have an authenticator code.
  1. 1
    Type the 6-digit code showing in your authenticator app.
  2. 2
    Click Sign in.

On the pilot: the password is culturals-demo and any 6-digit code works, for example 123456.

Finding your way

The menu on the left lists your pages. This is the Dean's menu. The Cultural Society and Management have the same pages except Venues.

  1. 1
    Approvals: proposals waiting for you. You start here.
  2. 2
    Venues: every booking and any clashes. Dean only.
  3. 3
    Calendar: every venue, hour by hour.
  4. 4
    This semester: running totals and what needs chasing.
  5. 5
    Semester report: attendance and money for the semester.

Your approvals queue

For all approvers

After signing in you land on your queue: every proposal waiting at your step.

  1. 1
    Click Review on a proposal to open it.

The three figures

Waiting is how many proposals are with you. Past SLA is how many have waited over 48 hours. Requested is the total money they ask for.

Tags on a card

Past SLA waited too long.
Venue clash another event wants the same hall.
Needs VC goes on to the Vice-Chancellor.

Only the late ones

Turn on Only items past the 48h SLA to see just the overdue proposals.

Same layout for everyone. In your menu it's called Society approvals, Dean approvals or Above-threshold (Management).

Review a proposal

For all approvers

Everything you need to decide is on one page. Read it from the top down.

  1. 1
    The route. Who has signed, who has it now, and who's next. Not needed means that approver is skipped for this event.
  2. 2
    Clash warning. Another event wants the same venue and time. It can't be approved until one event moves. Click Open the venue calendar to see both.
  3. 3
    Budget. Each line the club asks for. The Sanctioned column fills in once approved.
  4. 4
    Approval trail. Every earlier decision, with its date and comment.
  5. 5
    Your decision. At the bottom. See the next page.

The three boxes under the route

Requested: the total ask. Expected: how many people the club expects. At this gate: how long it has waited for you. Red means past 48 hours.

Decide

For all approvers

Every approver has the same three buttons. The club sees your decision and your comment.

  1. 1
    Comment. Optional to approve. Required to return or reject: say what to change.
  2. 2
    Return for changes. The club edits and sends it again. It restarts at the first step.
  1. 3
    Reject. Final. The proposal is closed.
  2. 4
    Approve. For the Cultural Society the button reads Approve and send to Dean.

The Dean: sanction by line Dean

The Dean also decides how much money each line gets. Every line starts at the amount asked for.

  1. 1
    Type a lower amount to cut a line. You can't raise it above what was asked.
  2. 2
    Add a comment if you like. Required to return or reject.
  3. 3
    Approve publishes the event, or sends it to the Vice-Chancellor if it's over ₹50,000 or part of a fest.

Management: the last step

For Management

Only the largest proposals reach Management: over ₹50,000, or part of a fest. By then the Cultural Society (if needed) and the Dean have approved, and the Dean has set the amounts.

  1. 1
    The route shows the Vice-Chancellor step whenever an event needs it.
  1. 2
    The reason is written under the amount, for example budget over 50000.

When it reaches you

It appears in your Above-threshold queue with the Needs VC tag. Click Review, check the approval trail (who approved before you, marked MFA when they signed in with the code), then use the same three buttons as on page 7. When nothing needs you, the queue says Nothing waiting at this gate.

Approve publishes the event at the amounts the Dean sanctioned.

Looking early

You can open a proposal before it reaches you. The bottom of the page then says which step it is waiting at, and there are no buttons until it's your turn.

Venues and clashes

For the Dean

When two events want the same hall at the same time, neither can be approved until one moves. The app never picks a winner: the Dean moves one, and the club sees the reason.

  1. 1
    In the red venue clash panel, click Move this one under the event you want to move.

Move an event

  1. 1
    Or click Move venue on any booking in the list.
  2. 2
    Move to: pick a venue. Free ones say free.
  3. 3
    Reason: the club sees this.
  4. 4
    Click Move.

Everyone affected is notified: the club's office bearers, the organisers and every registered student.

Calendar

For all approvers

Every venue, in one-hour slots. Use it to check a slot is free before you approve.

  1. 1
    Previous week, Today and Next week move between weeks.
  1. 2
    Click a day. The number is how many bookings it has. A red dot means a clash.

Reading a day

Each column is a venue, each row is an hour. A dashed box is a request still waiting for approval.

A red Clash cell is a slot two bookings share. Clashes are also listed in red above the grid.

Only the Dean can move an event, from Venues (page 9).

Two pages for keeping an eye on things. Nothing here needs a decision.

Quick answers

For all approvers
A proposal is marked Past SLA.
It has waited more than 48 hours at one step. Nothing is broken; it just needs attention.
I can't approve: there's a venue clash.
One of the two events has to move first. The Dean moves it from Venues.
I returned a proposal. What now?
The club edits it and sends it again. It starts from the first step, so earlier approvers see it again.
Can a rejected proposal come back?
No. The club has to propose a new event.
Why does this event need the Vice-Chancellor?
Its budget is over ₹50,000, or it's part of a fest. The proposal page says which.
Can the Dean give more than the club asked?
No. A line can be lowered, never raised.
Where do I get the 6-digit code?
From the authenticator app on your phone. Type the code showing at that moment.
